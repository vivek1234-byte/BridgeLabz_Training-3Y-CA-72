Walking the old salt road

The route runs for forty kilometres between two river towns and can be finished in three unhurried days. Most of it is flat, and the only real climb comes on the second afternoon.

Distance

40 km over three days, with short stages in the morning.

Season

October to February. Avoid the weeks right after the monsoon.

Stay

Two village guest houses and one forest rest house on the ridge.
