Why grid-template-areas is easier to read

4 March 2026 · 6 min read

Notes on spacing with gap

27 February 2026 · 4 min read

Margins collapse and fight each other. The gap property spaces grid and flex children evenly without any of that, which keeps the rhythm of a page consistent even when items are added or removed later.
