"It does not matter how slowly you go as long as you do not stop."

Quotes inspire people because they compress deep life lessons into a few memorable words. In moments of doubt or failure, a single powerful line from a great thinker can restore our confidence, remind us of our goals, and push us to keep moving forward. They connect us to the wisdom of people who faced great challenges and overcame them.
