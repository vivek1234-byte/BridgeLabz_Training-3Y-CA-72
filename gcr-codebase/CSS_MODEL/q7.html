Q7: The Art of Typography

Typography is the art and technique of arranging type to make written language legible, readable, and appealing when displayed. The arrangement of type involves selecting typefaces, point sizes, line lengths, line-spacing, and letter-spacing.

Good typography enhances the reader's experience. You can use a highlighted element to draw attention to specific parts of a sentence. Sometimes, words need strong emphasis to convey importance, while other times, a subtle italicized emphasis is enough to shift the tone of the sentence.

Achieving a visually balanced layout requires careful consideration of alignment, contrast, and spacing, guiding the user's eye effortlessly across the page.
