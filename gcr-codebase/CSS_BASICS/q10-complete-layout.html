About the studio

Harbour Lane started in 2021 as two people sharing a table above a print shop. The print shop is still downstairs, and we still walk proofs down the stairs rather than emailing them, which has saved us from more mistakes than any process document ever did.

We take on six to eight projects a year. That number is deliberate. It means the person you meet in the first conversation is the person doing the work, and it means we can say no to briefs we would do badly.

What we do

Websites

Design and build, usually four to eight weeks. We write the front-end ourselves and hand over something your team can edit without calling us.

Identity

Marks, type systems and the rules for using them. Delivered as a short guide people will actually read, not a sixty-page document.

Print

Books, packaging and signage. Proofed downstairs on the stock it will be printed on, because screens lie about paper.
